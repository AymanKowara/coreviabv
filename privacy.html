    Privacy statement

    Last updated: November 2026

    This website is operated by Corevia B.V. This statement explains how personal data is handled when you visit the site or contact us.

    What we collect

    The website itself does not collect personal data. It does not use analytics, tracking or advertising cookies. Your language choice is saved in your browser's local storage so the site opens in the same language next time; it never leaves your device.

    Hosting

    The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address to deliver the site securely.

    When you contact us

    The contact form does not send data to a server. It opens an email in your own mail app. When you send that email, we receive your name, email address and message and use them only to answer your enquiry. We do not keep them longer than needed for that purpose.
