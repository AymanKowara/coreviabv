# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, no framework. Deployed on GitHub Pages. (Confirmed by the developer, Sedra, in the init interview.)

## Users

- **Partners and investors** evaluating who Corevia B.V. is and whether it is a credible counterparty.
- **Clients of the group's operating companies** who land on the holding's site and need a way to reach the right contact.
- **Job seekers** looking for work within the group.

## Product Purpose

The corporate website of Corevia B.V., a holding company. It introduces the holding, gives each of the three audiences a clear path (partnership/investment enquiry, client enquiry routed through the holding, careers enquiry), and carries the legal company details expected of a Dutch B.V.

Success: a visitor from any of the three audiences understands what Corevia is and knows exactly how to contact it, in their own language.

## Positioning

Holding of three Dutch B.V.s (source: Corevia Business Plan, Nov 2026): Moventra B.V. (transport, through licensed partner carriers), Talentra B.V. (staffing/employment, Wtta authorisation in preparation) and Promovia B.V. (digital marketing). Founder and managing director: Ayman Kwara. Internal financial projections, risks and partner agreements from the plan are deliberately NOT published on the site.

## Operating Context

- The three operating companies are now named and described on the site (Nov 2026 update, from the business plan). Client enquiries still go through the holding's contact form, with a subject per company.
- Legal form B.V. implies Dutch registration; registered address, KvK number and VAT number are expected in the footer/imprint once supplied.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Seven languages: English, Dutch, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Polish (EN, NL, DE, FR, ES, IT, PL). Language switch must be available on every view.
- No back end. Any form must work on static hosting (mailto or a later-added form service).
- Undecided: registered address, KvK number, VAT number, contact email/phone, logo, founding year, sectors.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: **Corevia B.V.**, descriptor "Holding company".
- Binding constraint from the developer: clean, typography-driven design; no AI-generated or illustrative imagery.
- Standing choice (direction round, 2026-11-25): the classic corporate holding look, played straight (navy and white, restrained serif display). The developer declined to name reference sites; the craft bar is established Dutch and European holding-company sites.

## Evidence on Hand

None yet. No logo, figures, portfolio, team, testimonials, press, or photography. Do not fabricate any of these. Legal details stay as clearly marked placeholders until the client supplies them.

## Product Principles

1. Credibility through restraint: say only what is true and verifiable.
2. Every audience gets a direct path to contact within one scroll of finding their section.
3. All seven languages are first-class; no language is a partial translation.
4. Legal transparency: company details are easy to find.
